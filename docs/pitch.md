# Reliefy Pitch

**One-liner:** Reliefy is an AI-powered food procurement copilot for disaster-relief coordinators.

**Problem:** During disasters, coordinators must quickly turn scattered restaurant offers into reliable meal coverage under budget and deadline constraints.

**Solution:** Reliefy structures incoming offers, matches feasible options using transparent rules, and highlights coverage gaps for human verification.

**Tagline:** From local kitchens to disaster shelters — faster.

**Safety boundary for the prototype:** No real payments, dispatch, or outreach. All offers are simulated and recommendations require confirmation.
